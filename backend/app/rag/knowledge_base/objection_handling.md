# NovaFlow AI — Objection Handling Playbook

## Common B2B Prospect Objections & Approved Responses

### Objection 1: "It's too expensive / We don't have budget right now."
- **Acknowledge & Reframe:** Understand budget scrutiny—especially with software consolidation.
- **Value Proof:** Customers on the Growth plan ($99/seat/mo) typically save 6–8 hours per rep per week on manual CRM hygiene and follow-ups, paying back the license in under 45 days through a 18%–24% lift in qualified pipeline conversion.
- **Commercial Flexibility:** Offer a 14-day guided pilot or quarterly ramp pricing so teams only pay for active seats during rollout, plus waived $2,500 onboarding on annual agreements.

### Objection 2: "We already use Salesforce / HubSpot native workflows or Gong."
- **Acknowledge:** Salesforce Flow and Gong are strong systems of record and recording tools.
- **Differentiate:** NovaFlow AI sits *between* live buyer conversations and your CRM—actively executing multi-turn BANT qualification, booking calendar slots in real time, and writing clean structured fields back into Salesforce/HubSpot automatically without reps lifting a finger.

### Objection 3: "Implementation will take too long and drain our RevOps team."
- **Reassure with Proof:** Average go-live is under 10 business days. Connecting HubSpot or Salesforce takes 15 minutes via OAuth 2.0 with read/write field mapping templates, and our onboarding specialist handles playbook migration at zero extra fee on Growth and Enterprise.

### Objection 4: "We have strict InfoSec / SOC 2 / AI data privacy requirements."
- **Technical Proof:** NovaFlow AI is SOC 2 Type II certified, GDPR compliant, encrypts all payloads with AES-256/TLS 1.3, enforces automatic PII redaction, and never trains third-party foundation models on customer call data. Our security packet and DPA are available during technical evaluation.

### Objection 5: "Send me an email / I need to talk to my VP first."
- **Advance Gently:** Offer to send a tailored 1-page ROI summary and suggest a brief 20-minute technical walkthrough with their VP/RevOps lead so they don't have to translate technical details alone.
