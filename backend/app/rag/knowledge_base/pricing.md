# NovaFlow AI — Pricing & Packaging Guide

NovaFlow AI is a B2B SaaS Revenue & Workflow Automation Platform built for modern Sales, RevOps, and Customer Success teams.

## Subscription Tiers (Annual Billing)

### 1. Starter Plan — $49 / user / month
- **Best for:** Early-stage startups and small sales pods (5–25 seats).
- **Minimum:** 5 seats ($245/month billed annually, or $59/seat/mo billed monthly).
- **Included:**
  - Up to 2,500 automated CRM syncs & workflow runs/month
  - Native bi-directional HubSpot or Pipedrive sync
  - AI Call Summaries & Email Drafts
  - Email & live-chat support (24h SLA)

### 2. Growth Plan — $99 / user / month (Most Popular)
- **Best for:** Scaling B2B revenue teams (20–250 seats).
- **Minimum:** 10 seats ($990/month billed annually, or $119/seat/mo billed monthly).
- **Included:**
  - Unlimited automated workflows and lead routing
  - Bi-directional Salesforce, HubSpot, Slack, and Gong/Zoom integrations
  - Real-time AI BANT qualification & forecasting signals
  - Role-based access control (RBAC) & custom playbooks
  - Dedicated Slack channel + onboarding specialist (included free)
  - 99.9% uptime SLA

### 3. Enterprise Plan — Custom Pricing (Starts ~$25,000 / year)
- **Best for:** Mid-market and Enterprise orgs (100+ seats) with strict compliance needs.
- **Included:**
  - Volume seat discounts (15%–30% off Growth list rates)
  - SAML 2.0 SSO (Okta, Azure AD) & SCIM provisioning
  - SOC 2 Type II, GDPR DPA, HIPAA BAA, and EU/US data residency options
  - Snowflake & BigQuery zero-ETL warehouse sync
  - Custom AI fine-tuning on historical win/loss transcripts
  - Dedicated Solutions Architect & 99.99% uptime SLA

## Pilot & Implementation Terms
- **14-Day Guided Pilot:** Available for qualified Growth/Enterprise prospects with sandbox CRM data.
- **Implementation Fee:** Waived on annual Growth & Enterprise contracts ($2,500 value).
- **Average Time to Value:** Under 10 business days from kickoff to production go-live.
